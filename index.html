Before you ask.

Message us and a person replies, usually within two hours.

Can my WhatsApp number get banned?

CIPHER replies to customers who message you first; it doesn't cold-message strangers from your main number. Messages are paced like a person types, and outreach runs on a separate number with a strict daily limit.

If I cancel, what happens to my data?

You can export your leads and settings as a CSV before you go, so your customer list stays yours.

If CIPHER shuts down, does it stop working?

It keeps working, because it runs on your own Google Cloud account rather than ours.

Who can see my customer data?

Only you and your assigned CIPHER admin. It isn't shared, sold, or used to train anything outside your own setup.

What if it gives a wrong answer?

It only quotes prices and policies you've entered. If it doesn't know, it says so and hands the chat to you.

How do I get support?

Message us on WhatsApp. We watch uptime and reach out first if something goes down.

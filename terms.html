1. What CIPHER Is

CIPHER provides an AI-powered WhatsApp assistant that connects to your own WhatsApp number and replies to your customers automatically, based on the services, pricing, FAQs, and settings you configure in your CIPHER dashboard.

2. How the Bot Connects to WhatsApp — Please Read

The bot connects using an independent, unofficial connection method rather than Meta's official WhatsApp Business API. You should know that:

  • WhatsApp's own Terms of Service do not authorize this connection method, and WhatsApp may detect and temporarily restrict, or in rare cases permanently ban, a number using it — regardless of how it's operated.
  • We use real technical safeguards to reduce this risk (human-like reply delays, strict rate limits, no bulk or cold messaging on your main business number) but we cannot guarantee WhatsApp will never restrict a number.
  • If your number is restricted, we are not responsible for lost business during that time, but we will help you reconnect the bot to a new number as quickly as possible at no extra setup cost.

If your plan includes outreach (the bot proactively messaging potential new customers), it is limited to contacts with a genuine existing relationship to your business, capped at your plan's daily volume, sent only during normal daytime hours, and every message includes an easy opt-out. Anyone who opts out is never messaged again. You are responsible for making sure any contact list you provide was obtained lawfully.
